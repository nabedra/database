## Common transition and authority rules

All proposed APIs below are crate-private with private fields, value-free Debug and no Clone/Serde proof constructors. Use existing StorageError categories. Every change validates the complete base, captures its OwnershipObservation, requires the exact held Owner, checks a fresh proposal distinct from the base change UUID, increments root revision with checked arithmetic and bounded-encodes the complete candidate. Preserve controller, policy including its independent revision, admin sequence/head, config, Template, Timing, unrelated records and scheduler fields except A19's explicit failure/backoff updates. Check new IDs against retained identity roles as A19 requires. Do not prune history.

At final submission, the later caller observes the fresh complete root and invokes `guard.authorize(captured_generation, observation, fresh_paired_time)` under the short authority lock. Require the candidate Owner and original absolute deadline. Encoding, hashing, parsing and cloning run on the bounded coordinator CPU/I/O executor outside locks. No lock spans storage/callback awaits. A captured-token race fences a stale control write; a lease deadline alone does not fence a submitted control or database CAS.

The consumed lifetime is candidate plus captured base, one Match, at most one bounded reconciliation GET, then settlement. Reuse OwnershipOutcome. Only timely nonempty-token success is Acknowledged; whole candidate/change UUID equality can be ObservedExact. Definite rejection remains Conflict. Later roots, missing/failed/corrupt reads and ambiguous delivery remain Unknown. No automatic resend. A fresh-root/proposal CAS may recover within the original bounded budget, at most one fresh recovery proposal per invocation. Results go through guard observation rules, never ownership `finish` as if they were renewal evidence.

Durable `confirmed_idle` is retained metadata. A current local drain round also binds controller boot/Owner, guard generation, complete root, exact node/launch/resource/incarnation/session/verifier/drain and observed heartbeat sequence. Takeover/restart, guard invalidation, binding change or continuity failure discards that round. A new owner must freshly probe even if durable confirmed_idle is true. Ordinary metadata acknowledgements do not recreate it. Only the closed idle-confirmation, stop-allocation and stop-claim successor checks below can continue a proof to their exact acknowledged root. Any other newer root, including unrelated progress, makes check_for_stop return Conflict and requires a new authenticated round and heartbeat validation against that root. This deliberately conservative availability rule permits no arbitrary observation replacement and preserves the original round's time/authority limits through its allowed continuations.
